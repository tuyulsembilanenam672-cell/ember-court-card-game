Ember Court — an original duel card game. Two emberlords, four elements, one court. Flame beats Thorn, Thorn beats Tide, Tide beats Gale, Gale beats Flame; play two cards of one element in a turn and the second gains +1 power (Resonance). Spend ember to summon creatures and cast spells, break the Rival's board, and burn their 30 life to zero. 100% original art, rules, names and sounds — drawn on canvas, synthesized in WebAudio, zero network requests.

## Run it

```sh
cd game && python3 -m http.server 8080
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

## Play

- Click a card in your hand, then click the Rival's hero or one of their creatures to play it. Click one of your creatures, then a target, to attack.
- Keyboard: 1–7 select a hand card, ←/→ cycle targets, Space/Enter confirm, E end turn, M mute, R restart, Esc deselect.
- Each turn: +1 max ember (up to 10), refill, draw a card. Creatures attack once per turn. First to 0 life loses.
